Facilities & Dental Technology

Clinical Infrastructure

Ten Specialized Hospital Zones

Every area of DENTAL HOSPITAL is engineered around sterilization airflow, clinical ergonomic workflow, and patient psychological ease.

1. Dental Consultation Rooms

Acoustically isolated private consultation suites designed for thorough history taking, digital X-ray review on large high-definition medical displays, and patient counselling without operatory noise.

2. Advanced Dental Treatment Units

Twenty-five motorized dental operatories featuring memory-position memory chairs, high-output shadowless LED surgical lamps, integrated fiber-optic micromotors, and automated waterline disinfection purging.

3. Digital Dental Imaging Area

Equipped with intraoral digital radiovisiography (RVG) sensors offering ultra-high spatial resolution at up to 80% reduced radiation compared to traditional film, with instant chairside image projection.

4. Dental X-ray Facility (OPG & Cephalometric)

A lead-shielded diagnostic radiology room supporting Orthopantomogram (OPG) full-jaw panoramic scans and Lateral Cephalometric radiography vital for comprehensive orthodontic and surgical assessments.

5. Central Sterilization & Hygiene Department

A dedicated four-stage clean-to-dirty workflow: ultrasonic enzymatic pre-cleansing, automated pouch sealing, Class-B vacuum thermal sterilization, and sterile barrier cassette storage with biological verification.

6. Dental Implant Surgical Suite

A sterile operatory environment dedicated to surgical placement of titanium implants, bone grafting, and immediate temporization, supported by high-torque physiodispensers and sterile surgical draping.

7. Pediatric Dental Care Area

Specially designed operatory created to alleviate childhood dental anxiety. Features colorful wall motifs, child-proportioned dental furnishings, ceiling screens, and a relaxed environment for young patients.

8. Patient Waiting Lounge

A spacious, climate-controlled waiting area with sanitized seating arrangements, drinking water dispensers, oral hygiene educational displays, and separate seating for pediatric families.

9. Reception & Patient Registration Desk

Streamlined computerized check-in counters where care coordinators verify appointments, register medical histories, and guide patients to their scheduled specialty consultation rooms without delay.

10. Patient Support & Recovery Facilities

Post-operative observation recliners for surgical patients, wheelchair-accessible corridors, dedicated billing assistance desks, and oral medicine pharmacy support on-premises.
