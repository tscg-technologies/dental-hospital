Institutional Overview

Setting Higher Standards in Multi-Specialty Dental Care

DENTAL HOSPITAL was designed to transition dental medicine from isolated single-chair clinics into a modern, integrated hospital setting. Our facility hosts twelve fully coordinated dental wings, allowing patients to undergo cross-specialty diagnostic consultations, periodontal stabilization, endodontic therapy, and orthodontic rehabilitation without needing outside referrals.

Our clinical governance model centers on medical-grade infection prevention, computerized 3D imaging, and ethical, transparent patient counselling. Every treatment roadmap is formulated around the patient’s long-term masticatory function, oral aesthetics, and biological tooth retention.

Infection Control

Hospital Sterilization & Patient Safety Protocols

In healthcare, safety is non-negotiable. At DENTAL HOSPITAL, our infection prevention architecture mirrors international tertiary hospital regulations:

Class-B Fractional Vacuum Autoclaving

Sterilization of all hollow instruments, rotary handpieces, and surgical cassettes with integrated biological spore checks.

Single-Use Barrier Envelopes

Disposable sleeve barriers on light handles, sensors, headrests, and tubing, replaced fresh for every consultation.

Purified Waterline Filtration

Closed-circuit distilled water treatment bottles with anti-microbial cartridges preventing biofilm accumulation in chair lines.

Patient Experience

Patient Comfort, Care & Accessibility

A welcoming hospital environment designed to ensure stress-free visits for individuals of all age groups.

Universal Accessibility

Ramped wheelchair entrances, dedicated elevators, wide operatory corridors, and accessible restrooms ensure effortless mobility for elderly and specially-abled patients.

Anxiety-Free Dentistry

Our operatories are sound-damped and feature soothing ceiling displays, calming acoustic ambiance, and options for conscious nitrous oxide sedation when clinically indicated.

Clear Clinical Counselling

You will always receive a full explanation of treatment choices, visual display of digital X-rays, estimated treatment duration, and a written cost estimate prior to beginning any procedure.
