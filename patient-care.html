Preventive Wellness

General Dental Hygiene Guidance

Preserve your natural teeth and prevent periodontal complications with these essential daily oral habits.

Effective Brushing Technique

Brush twice daily for two full minutes using a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoridated toothpaste. Angle the bristles at 45 degrees toward the gum line using gentle circular vibrations rather than aggressive horizontal scrubbing.

Daily Interdental Cleaning

Toothbrushing cleans only 60% of tooth surfaces. Clean between your teeth daily using dental floss, interdental brushes, or water flossers to clear plaque from tight contact points where decay frequently begins.

Dietary Enamel Protection

Limit the frequency of sugary snacks, carbonated sodas, and acidic citrus juices. Minimize continuous sipping of sweetened tea or coffee, and rinse with plain water after consuming sticky carbohydrates.

Recovery Support

Post-Treatment Care Instructions

Standard guidelines to ensure smooth healing and optimal recovery following common dental procedures.

After Tooth Fillings

Wait until local anesthesia numbness wears off completely before eating to prevent accidental lip or tongue biting. Avoid chewing hard nuts on new restorations for the first 24 hours.

Standard Filling Care

After Tooth Extractions

Keep pressure on the gauze pad for 45 minutes. Do not spit, drink through a straw, or smoke for 24 hours to protect the healing blood clot. Eat soft, lukewarm foods and apply ice packs externally.

Surgical Recovery

After Root Canal Therapy

Mild soreness when biting is normal for 48 hours and responds well to prescribed analgesics. Avoid chewing on the treated tooth until the final permanent crown is placed to prevent structural fracture.

Endodontic Care

After Teeth Whitening

Adhere to a "white diet" for 48 hours: avoid coffee, black tea, red wine, turmeric curry, dark berries, and tobacco. Mild temperature sensitivity usually resolves within 24 to 48 hours.

Questions & Answers

Patient Care Frequently Asked Questions

Clear guidance on hospital policies, preparation, and follow-up care.

For acute dental trauma, severe facial swelling, or uncontrolled bleeding, please contact our demo triage line at +91 90000 00000. In case of systemic airway obstruction or unconsciousness, always visit the nearest general hospital emergency department immediately.

For routine consultations, cleanings, and simple fillings, having a light meal 1 to 2 hours prior is recommended. However, if you are scheduled for minor surgery or sedation procedures, follow the fasting instructions provided during your pre-operative assessment.

Non-urgent elective dental X-rays are generally postponed during pregnancy. However, if an acute dental infection requires diagnostic imaging, our low-radiation digital sensors paired with lead aprons and thyroid collars ensure maximum maternal and fetal safety.

Children should have their first dental visit around their first birthday or when their first tooth appears. Regular routine check-ups every 6 months allow early detection of milk tooth decay and monitoring of jaw development.
