Insurance Claims Support

Dental Insurance & Documentation Guidance

How dental reimbursement insurance works in India and how our hospital desk supports your claim paperwork.

Understanding Dental Coverage

In India, standalone outpatient dental visits are usually covered only if your health insurance policy includes an explicit Dental OPD Rider or Corporate Group Health Dental Benefit. Inpatient surgical hospitalizations (such as major jaw surgery following trauma) may be eligible under standard health policies.

Reimbursement Documentation

Our billing desk furnishes complete itemized invoices with GST registration, doctor clinical summary certificates, signed pre- and post-procedure radiographic records, and official hospital receipt stamps required by Third Party Administrators (TPAs).

Claim Limitations & Caveats

Cosmetic procedures such as teeth whitening, cosmetic veneers, and elective smile designing are typically excluded from health insurance reimbursement policies. Pre-authorization forms should be initiated by patients with their insurers prior to major surgical procedures.
